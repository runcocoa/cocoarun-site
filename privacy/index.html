Cocoa Run Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you play Cocoa Run, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, email support@cocoarun.com.

Who the game is for

Cocoa Run is for players aged 13 and over. When you first open the game we ask for your birth year. If you are under 13 you cannot play. Your birth year stays on your phone and is never sent to us. We only keep a note that you are 13 or over.

If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we delete it.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. As a guest, your progress is saved on your phone. We still receive crash reports and the game events described below, tied to a random id, not to your name or email.

What we collect

When you sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address, shared by Apple or Google when you sign in with them. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • An account id, and a username we generate for you. You cannot change the username at launch and it contains nothing about you.

Whether or not you sign in, we collect:

  • Your game progress: medals, best times, attempts per level, streak, daily goal, runs left, Hot Shots, Diamonds and outfits. Recordings of your best runs (ghosts) stay on your phone.
  • Your purchases: which plan or pack you bought and when. We use this to give you what you bought and to understand what sells. Apple handles payment. We never see your card details.
  • Game events: when a run starts, finishes, is quit or retried, and when a purchase is made. We use these to balance levels and fix problems.
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, your phone model and iOS version. These are not linked to your account.
  • A push token, if you turn on notifications, so we can send a streak reminder or a sale alert.
  • Bug reports you choose to send, with your phone model, app version, the level you were on and an image you select if you add one. For signed-in players, we store the report and image with the account. Guest reports have no account id.

We do not collect your precise location or contacts, or use your camera or microphone. If you choose to attach an image to a bug report, Apple's photo picker lets you select it; we receive only that image, not access to your photo library.

Ads

Cocoa Run offers optional rewarded ads from a third-party ad provider. To show, measure and verify an ad, the provider may collect device, ad and diagnostic data and an approximate location based on your IP address. If you are signed in, we also send your Cocoa Run account id so we can credit your reward. We request non-personalised ads.

Tracking

We do not track you. We do not link what we collect with other companies' data for advertising, and we do not sell or share your information with data brokers.

Who helps us run the game

We use third-party providers for accounts, sign-in, purchases, optional rewarded ads, crash reports, game analytics and notifications. They receive the data needed for those services and must protect it to the same standard described in this policy. Game events sent for analytics use a random id, with IP addresses discarded.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress while you have an account. Crash reports are kept for no longer than 90 days and then deleted. We keep bug reports and any attached images on our server for investigating and fixing issues. Deleting your account removes reports linked to it. Guest bug reports are not linked to an account and are not removed from our server when you delete the app. Guest data stored on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

Deleting your account

In the game go to Settings, Account, Delete account. After one confirmation we delete your account and progress from our servers straight away and sign you out. A plan you bought stays with your Apple Account: cancel it in your iPhone's subscription settings.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off notifications in the game's Settings or in your iPhone settings. You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@cocoarun.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights. We will not treat you differently for using them. We do not sell or share personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game.
